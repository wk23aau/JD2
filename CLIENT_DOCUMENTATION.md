## Introduction: What is the AI CV Maker?

Welcome to the AI CV Maker! This website is a powerful yet easy-to-use tool designed to help you create professional and effective resumes (also known as CVs). Whether you're applying for your first job, seeking a promotion, or changing careers, the AI CV Maker assists you in crafting a compelling document that showcases your skills and experience to potential employers.

Our goal is to simplify the often daunting task of resume writing. We provide you with the structure, suggestions, and a touch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) to make your CV stand out.

## Core Concepts: Understanding the Basics

To get the most out of the AI CV Maker, it's helpful to understand a few key ideas:

*   **User Accounts:**
    *   To save your work and access your CVs anytime, you'll need to create a personal account. This is like having your own private workspace on the website.
    *   You can sign up with your email and a password, or for even quicker access, you can use your existing Google account.
    *   Your account keeps your CVs secure and lets you come back to edit them whenever you need.

*   **CV Documents (Your Resumes):**
    *   A CV document is the actual resume you build using the website.
    *   You can create multiple CVs for different job applications, tailoring each one to the specific role you're targeting.
    *   Each CV you create is saved in your account, so you can easily find, update, or download it.

*   **Templates (Styling Your CV):**
    *   A template is a pre-designed layout and style for your CV. It determines how your information is presented, including the look, feel, and organization.
    *   We offer different templates (e.g., "Classic," "Modern") to suit various professional fields and personal preferences.
    *   You can choose a template when you start a new CV and can even switch templates later to see which one best fits your content.

*   **AI Assistance (Smart Suggestions):**
    *   "AI" stands for Artificial Intelligence. Our website uses AI to provide you with smart suggestions to help you write better content for your CV.
    *   For example, if you're stuck on how to phrase your job summary or describe your work experience, our AI can offer ideas and help you write clear, impactful statements.
    *   This is like having a helpful writing assistant by your side, making sure your CV is as strong as possible.

## Using the AI CV Maker: Step-by-Step

Here's how you can use the AI CV Maker to create and manage your CVs:

### 1. Getting Started: Accounts and Logging In

*   **Creating Your Account:**
    *   When you first visit the website, you'll have the option to "Sign Up."
    *   You can create an account by providing your email address and choosing a password.
    *   Alternatively, you can select "Sign in with Google" to use your existing Google account, which is often faster.
*   **Logging In:**
    *   Once you have an account, simply choose "Log In."
    *   Enter your email and password, or click "Sign in with Google" if that's how you registered.
*   **Why do I need an account?** Your account is your personal space where all your CVs and information are securely stored. You can log in from any computer to access your work.

### 2. Creating a New CV

There are a couple of ways to start a new CV:

*   **From the Landing Page:**
    *   Right on the main page, you might see an area where you can enter a "target job title" or "paste a job description." This is optional but can help the AI tailor suggestions for you later.
    *   After filling this (or leaving it blank), you can click a button like "Start Generating CV."
*   **Choosing a Template First:**
    *   The landing page (or a dedicated "Templates" page) will display various CV styles (our "Templates"). You can browse these (e.g., "Classic," "Modern").
    *   Clicking "Use Template" on your chosen style will also start the CV creation process.
*   **The CV Editor:**
    *   Whichever way you start, you'll be taken to the "CV Editor." This is your main workspace for building your CV.
    *   The editor will have different sections for you to fill in, such as:
        *   **Personal Information:** Your name, phone number, email, address, LinkedIn profile (if you have one).
        *   **Summary/Objective:** A brief paragraph about your career goals and what you offer. Our AI can help you write this!
        *   **Work Experience:** Details about your previous jobs – company name, job title, dates you worked there, and your responsibilities and achievements. The AI can also help you write strong bullet points for your achievements.
        *   **Education:** Information about your schooling and degrees.
        *   **Skills:** A list of your relevant skills (e.g., computer skills, languages, technical skills).
    *   As you fill in the information, you'll often see a preview of how your CV is looking with the selected template.

### 3. Managing Your CVs (Your Dashboard)

*   After you log in, you'll usually be taken to your "Dashboard."
*   The Dashboard is where you'll see a list of all the CVs you've created.
*   From here, you can:
    *   **View/Edit a CV:** Click on a CV in the list to open it in the CV Editor for more changes.
    *   **Delete a CV:** If you no longer need a CV, you can remove it.
    *   (Potentially) **Download a CV:** Options to download your CV as a PDF or other formats might be available here or in the editor. *(Specific download functionality will be confirmed in future documentation updates)*.
    *   (Potentially) **Rename a CV:** Give your CVs clear names so you can easily find them (e.g., "CV for Marketing Role," "Technical CV").

### 4. Using AI Assistance

One of the most helpful features of the AI CV Maker is its built-in intelligence:

*   **Getting Suggestions:**
    *   When you're in the CV Editor, you might see buttons or options like "Get AI Suggestion" or a sparkle icon next to certain fields (like the Summary or Work Experience sections).
    *   For example, if you're working on your "Summary":
        *   You can provide some basic ideas, or even link it to a job description you pasted earlier.
        *   The AI will then generate a few summary options for you to choose from, edit, or use as inspiration.
    *   For "Work Experience":
        *   You can input your job title, company, and some notes about your responsibilities.
        *   The AI can help you write clear and impactful bullet points that highlight your accomplishments.
*   **How it Helps:** The AI is there to help you overcome writer's block, use strong action words, and tailor your CV content effectively. You are always in control and can edit any suggestions the AI makes.

### 5. Your User Profile

*   You'll have a "Profile" section (often accessible from the main menu or your dashboard).
*   Here, you can:
    *   View your account details (like your username and email).
    *   Update your username.
    *   (Potentially) Change your password if you signed up with email/password.

### 6. For Administrators: Site Management (Admin Dashboard)

*   The AI CV Maker website also includes a special section for administrators.
*   This "Admin Dashboard" is not accessible to regular users. It's designed for individuals who manage the website.
*   **Purpose:** The admin dashboard allows administrators to oversee the website's operation. This might include:
    *   Viewing overall usage statistics.
    *   Managing user accounts (e.g., assisting users with account issues, though not typically viewing their CV content for privacy reasons).
    *   Potentially managing website settings or content, like available CV templates.
*   Access to this area is restricted to maintain security and operational integrity.
